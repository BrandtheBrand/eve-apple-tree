# Park a seed

Use when a topic surfaces that you are **not** opening now — a door you walked past, an aside in someone
else's sentence, the question an apple just raised. Parking costs one line; losing it costs the idea.

---

**Paste this:**

> Park these as seeds, don't open them: <list them, however roughly>

---

**What the gardener does**

- Writes one `seed` note per topic: `tree_type: seed`, a short handle for a title, and at most the question
  that made you park it. **No `time`** — it hasn't happened yet.
- Adds `field:` only if the seed clearly belongs to a topic you are already growing; that decides which pool
  it lands in (its tree's foot, or the shared forest pool).
- Confirms in one line and gets out of the way. It does **not** research them, rank them, or turn them into
  leaves — a leaf claims an idea was had.

**What you do later**

A seed leaves the pool one of two ways, and both are yours to call:

- **Germinate** — you open it. It becomes a trunk or a leaf, gets a real `time`, and the pool shrinks by one.
- **Compost** — you decide it doesn't matter. Take any question worth keeping out of it, then delete it.

A pool that only grows is a to-do list with better lighting. Sweep it at `weekly-tend`.
